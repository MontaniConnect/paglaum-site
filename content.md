## About

PAGLAUM — Philanthropic Development Office of the Missionaries of the Assumption — serves as the channel of resources of God's blessings for the various ministries and apostolates of the Missionaries of the Assumption.

Founded in 1989 and based in Davao City, we carry that mission into the field — **disaster relief, livelihood support, Lumad education, and advocacy for the poor, for women and children, and for the Earth.**

## Programs

heading: Our programs

### Duyog

tag: Duyog · Disaster response
title: Relief and solidarity when it matters most
image: pillar-duyog.jpg
link_label: Learn more on Facebook

Run in partnership with Assumption College of Davao and Assumption Polytechnic College in Southern Mindanao, DUYOG has brought food packs, drinking water, temporary shelter materials, medical assistance, and psychosocial support to families affected by the Sarangani earthquake, the Davao Oriental earthquake, and Tropical Storm Basyang.

### Pegtanggew

tag: Pegtanggew · Livelihood & environment
title: Nurturing the Earth & the Matigsalog community
image: pillar-pegtanggew.jpg
link_label: Learn more on Facebook

Pegtanggew — a Matigsalog word for "nurturing" — supports the Matigsalog indigenous community of Sitio Toruyan, Marilog District. Its work includes a cacao nursery for 69 farming families, ginger-seedling livelihood support, and a clean water project bringing life-giving water to the community.

### Lumad Education

tag: Lumad education · Balay Sibol
title: A safe place for volunteer teachers to serve
image: pillar-lumad.jpg
link_label: Learn more on Facebook

PAGLAUM supports the Grade School Extension for Lumad Children in Kibalatong, Brgy. Pañalum, Paquibato District. The Balay Sibol campaign raises funds to build a Volunteer Teachers' Cottage and School Library, so volunteer teachers have a safe, dignified space to rest, collaborate, and nurture Lumad children.

### Walis

tag: Walis · Social enterprise
title: Handcrafted brooms, sustaining heritage
image: pillar-walis.jpg
portrait: true
link_label: Learn more on Facebook

WALIS brooms are handcrafted by the Matigsalog community from 100% natural materials using traditional weaving techniques. Every ₱150 broom sold helps sustain indigenous livelihoods while preserving cultural heritage.
